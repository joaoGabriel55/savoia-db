# Savoia Studio site: landing + docs

Scope: the public site on GitHub Pages. Landing (`docs/site/landing/index.html`, Persuade) at the root; mdBook docs restyled under `/docs/` (Read).

Audience: backend developers on Postgres/MySQL deciding whether to try another client. Job: understand it in seconds, believe it is truly free, download it, and later support it on Ko-fi.
Proof on hand: real captures of the app on the Serie A sample, measured benchmarks in `docs/benchmarks/` (macOS, M3 Pro), MIT/Apache license, no account or telemetry (`privacy.md`).
Constraints: no invented users, testimonials, prices or numbers; benchmarks cite machine and method. Savoy blue is the one action (Download). Red only for danger.
Donation tone: warm and plain. Ko-fi in nav, hero, a short maker note, docs sidebar and footer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page owns one fact, the price is zero with nothing attached, set as a price tag on a drenched Ivrea field. It refuses the split hero over a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched Ivrea green first viewport in band ink; graphite sections below ruled with hairlines; Savoy blue fills only Download; Archivo (expanded, heavy) for display, system sans for reading, mono only for keys and commands; square bands, 6px controls; a terms ledger ruled in band-rule.

STORY: A native Postgres/MySQL client, genuinely free (no trial, key, account, telemetry), shown working in real captures of the daily loop; download it; if it saves you time, buy the maker a coffee.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width green field. Band nav on top (mark, Docs, GitHub, Ko-fi). Left a giant 0 at about 45vh; right the H1, a struck ledger of what you do not need, the blue OS-detected Download and Ko-fi. The real app window rises from the bottom edge, cropped by the fold.

FORM: Price tag, candidate 3 of 7 on my ordered list; seed key 2b25e2f0. Signature interaction: the ledger strikes draw through on load, once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
